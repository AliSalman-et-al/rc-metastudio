Results

Nineteen studies were included, with 1,097 participants in Intervention and 1,080 in Control. The pooled odds ratio for clinical failure was 0.770 (95% CI, 0.485 to 1.222; P = .267). The confidence interval includes associations in both directions. I² was 46.0% and τ² was 0.378 on the squared log odds-ratio scale. The 95% prediction interval was 0.212 to 2.799.

Methods

Odds ratios were pooled using a random-effects model with the DerSimonian–Laird estimator of between-study variance. Confidence intervals used the normal approximation. A continuity correction of 0.5 was applied to studies with any zero cell; two studies with no events in either arm were retained with this correction. Analyses used RC MetaStudio / RCMetaR 0.5.4, metafor 5.2.1, and meta 8.5-0.
