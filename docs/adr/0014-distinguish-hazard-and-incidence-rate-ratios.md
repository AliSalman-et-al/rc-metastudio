# Distinguish hazard and incidence rate ratios

RC MetaStudio will introduce distinct Time-to-event / Hazard ratio (HR) and Event rates / Incidence rate ratio (IRR) data types, preserving RR as risk ratio rather than reusing binary data for person-time. Initial HR inputs are reported HR with confidence interval or log(HR) with its standard error; IRR additionally accepts events and person-time for each group. Both measures must support every applicable analysis workflow under R-owned method eligibility and the existing statistical authority packages; HR reconstruction from log-rank statistics, Kaplan–Meier curves, or individual participant data is outside this release.

The existing one-outcome-family-per-project boundary is retained, so HR and IRR use separate projects. Both measures use generic inverse-variance fixed/random models across the existing applicable workflows; raw IRR additionally supports Mantel–Haenszel pooling, cumulative analysis, leave-one-out, and within-subgroup summaries, while Poisson GLMM is deferred because it needs distinct workflow and result handling. Mantel–Haenszel does not supply a moderator model or an implied subgroup-difference test.

Pooled estimates, predictions, and forest plots show ratios with logarithmic geometry; regression retains log coefficients and their SEs and additionally presents exponentiated coefficients as multiplicative changes in HR/IRR. Small-study effects deliberately enables classical Egger as primary under the existing R-owned study-count and precision rules, with Begg and mixed-effects Egger exploratory and trim-and-fill optional, preserving the existing meta authority and interpretation limits.
