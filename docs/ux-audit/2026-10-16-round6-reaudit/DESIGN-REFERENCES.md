# External design references

Consulted 15 October 2026. These inform recommendations, not statistical or native
verification receipts.

- [Cochrane chapter 6](https://training.cochrane.org/handbook/current/chapter-06):
  distinguish the analysis scale of ratio measures from their reported ratios.
- [RevMan DTA graphs](https://documentation.cochrane.org/revman-kb/analysis-graphs-in-dta-reviews-236847627.html):
  separate forest and SROC settings, with confidence/prediction display controls.
- [RevMan manual input](https://documentation.cochrane.org/revman-kb/manual-input-analyses-259686817.html):
  concise footnotes that remain fully visible.
- [RevMan predefined analyses](https://documentation.cochrane.org/revman-kb/pre-define-analyses-260702251.html):
  explicit prediction-interval display, numerical and graphical, for totals.

Applied judgement: keep natural-scale values and fitted-scale inference clearly
labelled, give raw-data columns meaningful headings, keep figure footers legible,
and make controls describe the actual displayed artifact. A RevMan label does
not imply pixel-identical rendering or qualification against RevMan's engine.
