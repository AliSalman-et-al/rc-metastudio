# Draft conclusions to reconcile against the completed sweep

The release candidate is materially better than round five in data integrity and
statistical presentation. The important distinction is that calculator formatting
is now presentation rather than a model write. Selected diagnostic precision,
fitted SE-scale labels, meaningful defaults, measured long-name headers and
row-sized caps remove multiple sources of avoidable interpretation work.

The axis policy is coherent: ratios retain log spacing and natural labels with
an explicit opt-in linear projection; proportions and diagnostic Sens/Spec forests
use natural linear 0–1 coordinates; differences stay linear. A coordinate choice
must not imply a different fit. Proportion regression bubbles are an intentional
exception: fitted transformed coordinates remain labelled as such. Funnel/SROC
axes retain their family conventions. This distinction belongs in the final report.

The independent PFT review covers all three styles and actual standard,
cumulative, omission and subgroup paths. Values in the footer/Table/export agree
with public package inverse checks. Removing meaningless single-arm spanners is
right; R6-01 requests the semantic raw-column label that BMJ still lacks.

Do not claim the release binary or every platform is qualified from these native
source drives. About and freshly saved metadata actually show 0.5.0. The changelog
matches inspected round 3–6 changes; its portable Ubuntu download, TIFF benchmark
and cross-platform distribution claims require their own retained release/benchmark
records rather than a statistical GUI pass.

Final verdict remains pending completed registry, visual matrix and failures.
