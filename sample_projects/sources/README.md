# Retained sample sources

- **Colditz 1994.** The 13 former RCMS rows match OpenMeta[Analyst] on study label, year, both event/total pairs and latitude. The pinned `metadat::dat.bcg` agrees on all 13 years and event/total pairs after matching by the published arm direction and allowing for display-name and row-order differences. The rebuilt project retains the package's study names, order, latitudes and allocation factor. [Arm direction and original comparison](bcg-source.json) · [Retained table](metadat-clinical.json)
- **Barza 1996.** The former RCMS archive matches OpenMeta[Analyst] on 19/19 rows per outcome. Both event/total pairs for each of these 38 comparisons match page 340, Table 2, in single-dose versus multiple-dose order. Page 343 supplies the years. The builder uses the legacy transcription's study spelling, including Maller for Mäller. The number of episodes supplies the clinical failure denominators when that is what the paper reports. [Retained rows and primary tables](barza1996.json)
- **Poole 2003.** `metadat::dat.poole2003` retains all 19 rows. The sample uses 17 with two positive SDs. Jackson 1984 and Meister 1999 report zero SD in both arms. Each selected row retains year, duration, and both arms' N, mean exacerbations per month and SD. [Retained table](metadat-clinical.json)
- **Kearon 1998.** `metadat::dat.kearon1998` retains 34 source rows. The sample uses 27 complete tables, with FN = cases − TP and FP = noncases − TN. Case status was obtained using contrast venography. Some rows count limbs. See the overlap check below. [Retained table](metadat-clinical.json) · [Package documentation](https://wviechtb.github.io/metadat/reference/dat.kearon1998.html)
- **Greenland 1992.** All 16 `metadat::dat.greenland1992` study labels, years, adjusted coefficients and SEs are retained. The CI sheet represents the same coefficients using derived 95% Wald bounds. The estimate is a log risk ratio per additional gram of alcohol per day. [Retained table](metadat-clinical.json)
- **Abe 2025.** Five independent general-volunteer cohorts from Abe Table 3 match primary results and total 235 participants. Each row documents the measurement, the table, and any conversion or rounding. Cildan Uysal 2022 was excluded because the accessible primary text omits the first-session mean and SD. Gerodimos 2012 was excluded because it enrolled adult male basketball players. Direct `metafor::rma(measure="MN")` gives 38.57757297 kg (95% CI 37.09385785 to 40.06128808) by FE, 40.76405947 kg (36.12932490 to 45.39879403) by DL, and 40.78693921 kg (35.99286703 to 45.58101139) by REML. [Retained rows and primary DOI links](abe2025.json)
- **Feng 2019.** `metadat::dat.feng2019` has 19 rows. The 16 observational rows retain the study label, year, design, events and totals. The three randomized-study rows remain in the source. [Retained table](metadat-clinical.json)
- **Niel-Weise 2008 and Pignon 2000.** All nine IRR and 65 HR rows are retained from pinned `metadat`. The IRR inputs are the published event counts and catheter-days. For HR, log(HR) = O−E divided by V, and SE = 1/√V. Years were unavailable, so the project retains original trial identifiers and table order. [Retained tables](metadat-ratios.json)

## Kearon overlap check

Four incomplete tables lack necessary counts (source row IDs 2, 18, 27 and 34). Within each group the selected tables have distinct study publications. Across the two groups, Mattos 1992 reports separately recruited symptomatic and postoperative cohorts, which are labeled explicitly in the sample. [Mattos primary study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1735897/)

The asymptomatic subset also omits Barnes 1991 (ID 22), identified as a duplicate report in Kassaï et al., and two tables with unresolved overlap signals. Borris 1989 (ID 20) has authors and a postoperative setting in common with Borris 1990 (ID 21). Elliot 1993 (ID 30) has shared investigators and a postoperative setting with Davidson 1992 (ID 28). The two Woolson series enrolled consecutive patients after different operations, hip replacement in 1990 and knee arthroplasty in 1991. The remaining asymptomatic subset has 11 tables. The package's Deeks example includes more tables, so the comparison uses only the retained counts. [Kassaï review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2706694/) · [Woolson 1990](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2200790/) · [Woolson and Pottorff 1991](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1959260/) · [Kearon dataset and Deeks example](https://wviechtb.github.io/metadat/reference/dat.kearon1998.html)

## Earlier samples

The pinned-library search loaded 110 `metadat` tables, 13 `meta` tables and six `mada` tables. It compared study labels, years and raw values. The OpenMeta[Analyst] archives were compared against the old RCMS rows. [OpenMeta[Analyst] corpus at the inspected revision](https://github.com/bwallace/OpenMeta-analyst-/tree/e3147cab25e773251e7052f3bf27852ea41d522e/sample_data)

For a single-arm mean, the search screened `dat.meeker2017` (baseball), `dat.curtin2002` (crossover contrasts), `dat.senn2013` (comparative trial arms) and `dat.lloyd2010` (changes with confidence bounds). Rees's hospital-stay supplement yielded two independent general-stay rows with complete data. The RIMeta postural vertical example reproduces Conceição's published supplement but includes repeated cohorts and healthy controls from comparative studies. Abe provides five independent first-session cohorts with verified means and SDs. [Rees et al.](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12916-020-01726-3) · [Conceição et al.](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0204122)

- The former mortality sample matched 65/65 labels, years and count pairs in OpenMeta[Analyst]. All 65 group assignments differed. `meta::Olkin1995` matched 21 of the 65 year/count tuples in the existing arm order and zero in reverse. `metadat::dat.lau1992` shared nine first-token labels. These comparisons did not establish clinical provenance for the full sample.
- The former blood pressure sample matched six labels, years and both summaries in OpenMeta[Analyst]. The same six labels and summaries appear in Borenstein et al., Table 14.1, which omits clinical provenance and study years. [Textbook chapter](https://doi.org/10.1002/9780470743386.ch14)
- The former lymphangiography sample matched 17/17 OpenMeta[Analyst] rows and 17/17 Holling Table B15 rows on year and all four cells. Six labels differ in spelling or attribution. Kearon provides the diagnostic subgroup without pooling CT and lymphangiography tables of uncertain independence. [Holling appendix](https://www.statmod.org/smij/Vol12/Iss4/Holling/Data.pdf)
- The earlier synthetic mean sample was replaced with the reconciled Abe cohorts.
