clinical failure: fixed-effect meta-analysis (risk difference) Comparison: Intervention / Control Methods Risk difference estimates were pooled using a fixed-effect model. A continuity correction of 0.5 was applied to individual study estimates only; the pooled Mantel-Haenszel estimate uses the original counts. Heterogeneity was assessed with Cochran's Q and I². Analyses were performed in RC MetaStudio 0.5.1. R packages loaded for this analysis: metafor 5.0.1, meta 8.5-0. Results 19 studies were included (1097 participants in Intervention, 1080 in Control). The pooled risk difference was -0.030256 (95% CI [-0.055844, -0.004668], z = -2.317520, p = .020). The confidence interval excludes 0, with lower risk in Intervention than in Control. Heterogeneity: Q = 43.705892, df = 18, p < .001. I² = 65.7%, which may represent substantial heterogeneity (Cochrane Handbook ranges). Table 1.
