clinical failure: fixed-effect meta-analysis (odds ratio)

Comparison: Intervention / Control

Methods

Odds ratios were pooled using a fixed-effect model. A continuity correction of 0.5 was applied to individual study estimates only; the pooled Peto estimate uses the original counts. Heterogeneity was assessed with Cochran's Q and I². Analyses were performed in RC MetaStudio 0.5.1. R packages loaded for this analysis: metafor 5.0.1, meta 8.5-0.

Results

19 studies were included (1097 participants in Intervention, 1080 in Control). The pooled odds ratio was 0.710 (95% CI [0.541, 0.930], z = -2.482, p = .013). The confidence interval excludes 1, with lower odds of the outcome in Intervention than in Control. Heterogeneity: Q = 41.000, df = 18, p = .002. I² = 56.1%, which may represent moderate to substantial heterogeneity (Cochrane Handbook ranges).
