clinical failure: fixed-effect meta-analysis (odds ratio)
Comparison: Intervention / Control
Methods
Odds ratios were pooled using a fixed-effect model. A continuity correction of 0.5 was applied to individual study estimates only; the pooled
Mantel-Haenszel estimate uses the original counts. Heterogeneity was assessed with Cochran's Q and I². Analyses were performed in RC
MetaStudio 0.5.1. R packages loaded for this analysis: metafor 5.0.1, meta 8.5-0.
Results
19 studies were included (1097 participants in Intervention, 1080 in Control). The pooled odds ratio was 0.712 (95% CI [0.543, 0.934], z =
-2.455, p = .014). The confidence interval excludes 1, with lower odds of the outcome in Intervention than in Control. Heterogeneity: Q =
33.492, df = 18, p = .006. I² = 52.2%, which may represent moderate to substantial heterogeneity (Cochrane Handbook ranges).
