Methods

We pooled odds ratios (OR) using a random-effects model with the REML estimator of between-study variance and the Wald z statistic. Studies were added in table order. The required information size was 117,364 participants using a two-sided test with α = 0.05 and 80% power. The anticipated intervention effect was an OR of 0.6835, the 95% CI limit closest to the null (Wetterslev 2017). The control event proportion was 4.49% (unweighted mean of control arms). The information size was adjusted for D² = 94.8%. Monitoring boundaries used Lan-DeMets O’Brien-Fleming spending. Futility boundaries were non-binding. The penalised Z-curve used the law of the iterated logarithm with λ = 2 (Hu 2007). Sensitivity analyses repeated the analysis with alternative anticipated intervention effects. The analysis used metafor 5.2-1 and gsDesign 3.11.0.

Results

The cumulative Z-curve crossed the monitoring boundary favouring BCG vaccine when Hart & Sutherland was added (27,787 participants, 24% of the required information size). Accrued information is 304% of the required information size. The conventional 95% CI was 0.3296 to 0.6835. A TSA-adjusted CI is not reported because the required information size was reached. The final penalised Z-score was -2.085. Because the cumulative Z-curve crossed the monitoring boundary, the result remains statistically significant after accounting for repeated significance testing.
