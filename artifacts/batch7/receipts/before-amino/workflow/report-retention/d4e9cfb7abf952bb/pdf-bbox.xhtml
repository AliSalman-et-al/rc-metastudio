clinical failure: random-effects meta-analysis (odds ratio) Comparison: Intervention / Control Methods Odds ratios were pooled using a random-effects model with the DerSimonian–Laird estimator of between-study variance. Confidence intervals (95%) used the normal approximation. A continuity correction of 0.5 was applied to studies with any zero cell. 2 studies with no events in either arm were retained with this correction. Heterogeneity was assessed with Cochran's Q and I². Analyses were performed in RC MetaStudio 0.5.1. R packages loaded for this analysis: metafor 5.0.1, meta 8.5-0. Results 19 studies were included (1097 participants in Intervention, 1080 in Control). The pooled odds ratio was 0.770 (95% CI [0.485, 1.222], z = -1.111, p = .267). The confidence interval includes 1, so these data do not show a clear difference between Intervention and Control. Heterogeneity: Q = 33.360, df = 18, p = .015. I² = 46.0%, which may represent moderate heterogeneity (Cochrane Handbook ranges). Between-study variance was τ² = 0.378. The 95% prediction interval was 0.212 to 2.799. Table 1.
