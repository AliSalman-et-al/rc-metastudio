blood pressure: random-effects meta-analysis (standardized mean difference)

Comparison: Intervention / Control

Methods

Standardized mean differences were pooled using a random-effects model with the DerSimonian–Laird estimator of between-study variance. Confidence intervals (95%) used the normal approximation. Heterogeneity was assessed with Cochran's Q and I². Analyses were performed in RC MetaStudio 0.5.1. R packages loaded for this analysis: metafor 5.0.1, meta 8.5-0.

Results

6 studies were included (500 participants in Intervention, 495 in Control). The pooled standardized mean difference was 0.358 (95% CI [0.152, 0.565], z = 3.400, p < .001). The confidence interval excludes 0, with higher mean values in Intervention than in Control. Heterogeneity: Q = 11.914, df = 5, p = .036. I² = 58.0%, which may represent moderate to substantial heterogeneity (Cochrane Handbook ranges). Between-study variance was τ² = 0.037. The 95% prediction interval was -0.072 to 0.789.
