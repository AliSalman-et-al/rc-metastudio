Tuberculosis: random-effects meta-analysis (risk difference)

Comparison: BCG vaccine / Control

Methods

Statistical analyses were performed using RC MetaStudio (version 0.6.0; Research Consultancy) [1] with the R package metafor (version 5.2.1) [2]. Risk difference estimates were pooled using a random-effects model with the DerSimonian–Laird estimator of between-study variance [3]. Confidence intervals (95%) used the normal approximation. Heterogeneity was assessed with Cochran’s Q and I².

Results

In total, 13 studies were included (191,064 participants in BCG vaccine, 166,283 in Control). The pooled risk difference was −0.01 (95% CI −0.01 to 0.00; z = −4.51; P < 0.001). Heterogeneity: Q = 276.47 (df = 12; P < 0.001); I² = 95.66%; τ² = 0.00. The 95% prediction interval was −0.02 to 0.00.
