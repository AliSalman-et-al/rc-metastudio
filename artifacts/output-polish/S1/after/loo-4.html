Tuberculosis: random-effects meta-analysis (risk ratio)

Comparison: BCG vaccine / Control

Methods

Statistical analyses were performed using RC MetaStudio (version 0.6.0; Research Consultancy) [1] with the R package metafor (version 5.2.1) [2]. Risk ratios were pooled using a random-effects model with the DerSimonian–Laird estimator of between-study variance [3]. Confidence intervals (95%) used the normal approximation. Leave-one-out analyses refitted the model after omitting each study in turn. Each fit’s pooled estimate and confidence interval are reported, with I² where the package reports it.

Results

In total, 13 studies were included (191,064 participants in BCG vaccine, 166,283 in Control). With all studies, the pooled risk ratio was 0.4896 (95% CI 0.3449 to 0.6950; I² = 92.1%). Across all 13 omission fits, pooled estimates ranged from 0.4538 (omitting TB Prevention Trial, 1980) to 0.5386 (omitting Hart & Sutherland, 1977). I² after omission ranged from 83.8% (omitting TB Prevention Trial, 1980) to 92.8% (omitting Coetzee & Berjak, 1968). Table 1 gives every omission fit.
