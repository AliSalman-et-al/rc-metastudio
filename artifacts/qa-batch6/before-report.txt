Clinical interpretation
Across 17 studies, summary sensitivity was 65.762% (95% confidence interval 59.020% to 71.923%) and summary specificity was 80.975% (95% confidence interval 73.885% to 86.492%).
Clinical interpretation
These are average operating characteristics across the included study thresholds. The Reitsma bivariate and HSROC formulations are equivalent parameterizations of this model; that equivalence does not assume a common threshold. Use the prediction intervals to assess how performance may vary in a new setting.
