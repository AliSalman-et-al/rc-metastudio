clinical failure: fixed-effect meta-analysis (odds ratio)

Comparison: Intervention / Control

Methods

Odds ratios were pooled using a fixed-effect model. Leave-one-out analyses were performed by omitting each study in turn. A continuity correction of 0.5 was applied to individual study estimates only; the pooled Mantel-Haenszel estimate uses the original counts. Analyses were performed in RC MetaStudio 0.5.1. R packages loaded for this analysis: metafor 5.0.1, meta 8.5-0.
